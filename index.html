From the workbench

Gnomewright started the way most good workshops do, with one printer, a pile of paint, and too many ideas for one tabletop. Every wall, ruin, and dungeon tile starts as hard geometry in Fusion 360, then gets roughed up and given character in Blender, with cracks, moss, and weathering that make a wall look like it actually survived a siege.

Gnomewright is a solo gnome workshop based in the north woods, which means every piece that leaves the shop has actually been designed, printed, sanded, and painted by the same set of hands, not a warehouse and not a print farm.

What started as terrain for our own games is growing into something bigger, a full catalog of printable designs and a place for other builders to trade ideas, tricks, and encouragement.

Projects

A growing catalog of original terrain builds. Each one will be offered two ways, as a printable file and as a finished, hand painted piece.

Nothing shown here is available for purchase just yet. Both the printable files and the finished, hand painted pieces are being prepared for sale on Etsy soon.

Printed pieces may vary slightly from the photos shown and can include small imperfections that are a normal part of the three dimensional printing process.

Every finished piece is painted by hand, so each one is one of a kind and may look slightly different from the photos shown.

Most projects on this page are built to a rough one quarter inch scale. This is a rough estimate only, meant as a general guide for size and comparison rather than an exact measurement. Pieces are not designed to an exact scale.

Halloween Village

A featured collection of its own. A small, spooky town built to gather on one shelf, sold as individual pieces or as a full set.

The Halloween Village collection is built to a rough one eighth inch scale, smaller than the rest of the catalog so the full village can sit together. As with everything else on this page, this is only a rough estimate for sizing, not an exact measurement.

The same printing and painting notes above apply to every piece in this collection as well.
