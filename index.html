From the workbench

Gnomewright started the way most good workshops do — with one printer, a pile of paint, and too many ideas for one tabletop. Every wall, ruin, and dungeon tile begins life as a digital sketch before it's printed layer by layer and finished by hand.

We're a one-gnome operation based in the north woods, which means every piece that leaves the shop has actually been touched, sanded, and painted by the person who designed it — not a warehouse.

What started as terrain for our own games is growing into something bigger: a full catalog of printable designs, and a place for other builders to trade ideas, tricks, and encouragement.
